# Visual Inspection SOP (SOP-QA-014)

## Scope
Applies to in-process visual QA of P-series CNC housings and injection-moulded covers.

## Procedure
1. Read the work order ID stamped on the part face and record it on the traveler.
2. Inspect the part face under the inspection lamp for linear discontinuities.
3. Measure the part envelope and compare against the tolerance specification.
4. Classify the part as no_defect, surface_scratch, crack, or dimensional_deviation.
5. Apply the rework-vs-scrap rules. Propose the disposition; do not execute it.
6. Obtain supervisor sign-off before any part leaves the cell as scrap.

## Records
Every inspection must be logged with timestamp, work order ID, classification and the
inspector or system that made the call. Records are append-only and retained 7 years.
