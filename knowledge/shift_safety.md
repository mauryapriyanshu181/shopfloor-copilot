# Shift Safety and Handover

## Stop-the-line authority
Any operator or inspector may stop the line for a safety hazard or a suspected crack.
Press the andon button and tell the shift supervisor. No one is disciplined for stopping
the line in good faith.

## Lockout/tagout
Before reaching into any machine, isolate its power, fit your personal lock and tag, and
try the start button to prove zero energy. Only the person who fitted a lock removes it.

## Personal protective equipment (PPE)
Safety glasses are required everywhere on the shop floor. Wear cut-resistant gloves when
handling sharp-edged housings, and hearing protection within the marked zone around IM-02.

## Shift handover
Shifts change at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00. The outgoing supervisor records open escalations,
parts in quarantine and machines that are down in the handover log. The incoming
supervisor reads and signs it before the first cycle starts.

## Reporting injuries
Report every injury, however minor, to the shift supervisor straight away. The first aid
kit is at the inspection bench. For an emergency, call ext 4999.
