# Nonconformance Report (NCR) Process

## When an NCR is required
Raise an NCR for every part that is quarantined, scrapped, reworked, or released under
concession. A cosmetic scratch released as-is under Rule R2 needs only a traveler note,
not an NCR. A work order with an open NCR is put on hold in the ERP until the NCR is
dispositioned.

## NCR number and fields
NCR numbers use the format NCR-YYYY-NNNN, for example NCR-2026-0042. Each report records
the work order, part number, quantity affected, defect classification, a photo, who
detected it, and the containment action taken.

## Containment
Within one hour of raising an NCR, quarantine every suspect part and check the last ten
parts made on the same machine. If any of those also fail, widen the check to the whole
work order and inform the shift supervisor.

## Disposition
The inspector proposes one of four dispositions: use as-is under concession, rework,
scrap, or return to supplier. The Material Review Board approves it at its daily 10:00
meeting. Use-as-is always needs the Quality Manager's signature.

## Closing an NCR
An NCR is closed only after the root cause is found, a corrective action is in place,
and the fix is verified on the next production run. NCRs must close within 30 days;
overdue NCRs are reviewed weekly by the Quality Manager.
