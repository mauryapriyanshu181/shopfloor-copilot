# Tolerance Specifications

## Crack tolerance
No crack of any length is acceptable on a load-bearing CNC part. Any part classified
as `crack` is non-conforming and must be quarantined immediately. Do not attempt
rework on a cracked part; cracks propagate under load.

## Surface scratch tolerance
Surface scratches up to 0.4 mm depth and 25 mm length are cosmetic and acceptable on
non-sealing faces. Scratches on any sealing or mating face are non-conforming
regardless of depth. Scratches deeper than 0.4 mm require polishing rework.

## Dimensional tolerance
Nominal length-to-width aspect ratio for the P-series housing is 1.60 with a tolerance
band of +/- 0.08. Measured aspect outside 1.52 - 1.68 is a `dimensional_deviation`.
Deviations under 0.12 are reworkable by re-machining; above 0.12 the stock is gone and
the part must be scrapped.

## No defect
A part passing crack, scratch and dimensional checks is released to the next operation.
The inspector still records the work order ID on the traveler.
