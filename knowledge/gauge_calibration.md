# Gauge Calibration

## Calibration intervals
Calipers and micrometers are calibrated every 6 months. The height gauge and the
profilometer are calibrated every 12 months. The CMM is calibrated yearly and its probe
is checked every morning. The sticker on each gauge shows the next due date.

## Out-of-calibration or dropped gauges
If a gauge is past its due date, fails its daily check, or has been dropped, stop using
it and red-tag it. Every part measured with it since its last good calibration must be
re-inspected with a calibrated gauge, and the affected work orders need an NCR.

## Gauge tolerance (ten-to-one rule)
A gauge must be at least ten times finer than the tolerance it checks. A housing length
toleranced at plus or minus 0.05 mm needs a gauge that resolves 0.005 mm, which means a
micrometer, not a caliper.

## Daily zero check
At the start of every shift, zero each caliper and micrometer against the reference gauge
block and record the reading in the gauge log. A reading off by more than one division
means the gauge goes to the metrology lab.

## Metrology lab
The metrology lab, ext 4170, handles calibration, gauge repairs and any measurement
dispute between production and quality. Send gauges in the blue gauge case.
