# Rework vs Scrap Decision Rules

## Rule R1 - Crack
A part with a crack is always scrap. Never rework a crack. Raise a scrap ticket against
the work order and tag the material lot for review.

## Rule R2 - Surface scratch
A cosmetic surface scratch on a non-sealing face: release as-is, note on the traveler.
A surface scratch on a sealing face: route to polishing rework, max two rework attempts.

## Rule R3 - Dimensional deviation
If the measured dimension is above nominal, material remains and the part is reworkable
by re-machining. If the dimension is below nominal, material cannot be added - scrap.

## Rule R4 - Scrap authority
A scrap decision is never executed by the inspector or by an automated system. It is
proposed, then signed off by the shift supervisor. Scrap above 5 units in one work
order additionally requires the Quality Manager.
