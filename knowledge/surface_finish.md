# Surface Finish Specification (P-series Housing)

## Roughness limits
Surface roughness is measured with the profilometer and reported as Ra. Sealing faces
must be Ra 0.8 um or better, mating faces Ra 1.6 um, and all other faces Ra 3.2 um.
A face outside its Ra limit is non-conforming even if no scratch is visible.

## Sealing and mating faces
The P-series housing has three functional faces. Face A, the gasket face, and Bore B, the
O-ring bore, are sealing faces. Face C, the flange that bolts to the pump body, is a
mating face. Every other face is non-sealing. On the drawing, sealing faces carry the
letter S inside a circle.

## Polishing rework
Scratches on a sealing face are reworked by polishing: 600 grit, then 1200 grit, always
along the machining lay. Re-measure Ra after each attempt. Two attempts are allowed. If
the face still fails after the second attempt, propose scrap for supervisor sign-off.

## Tool marks vs scratches
Machining leaves regular tool marks that follow the cutter path. These are not defects
as long as the face meets its Ra limit. A mark that crosses the lay at an angle, or
appears on only one part in a batch, is a scratch and is judged by Rule R2.

## Burrs and edge break
All machined edges must be deburred to an edge break of 0.2 to 0.5 mm. A sharp burr left
on an edge is a handling hazard and is reworked at the bench with a deburring tool before
the part moves on.
