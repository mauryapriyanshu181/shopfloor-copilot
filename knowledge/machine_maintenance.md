# CNC Machine Maintenance (CNC-07)

## Signs of tool wear
A worn tool shows rising spindle load, chatter marks, burrs on exit edges, and dimensions
that drift the same way over consecutive parts. Any one of these means the tool should be
checked before the next cycle.

## Tool change intervals
On CNC-07 the roughing end mill is replaced every 400 parts, the finishing end mill every
250 parts, and drills every 600 holes. Record each change in the tool life log beside the
machine.

## Repeated dimensional deviation
Two or more dimensional deviations on the same machine within one shift point to tool
wear or thermal growth, not to bad parts. Stop adjusting offsets and call the Maintenance
Lead on ext 4155.

## Coolant concentration
Coolant concentration must stay between 6 and 8 percent. Check it at the start of each
shift with the refractometer. Weak coolant causes poor surface finish and thermal growth;
top up with premix, never with plain water.

## Spindle warm-up
Run the 15-minute warm-up program at the start of each shift and after any stop longer
than two hours. Measure the first part after warm-up in full before running the batch.

## Collisions
If the tool or spindle strikes the vise, fixture or part, press emergency stop and do not
restart. Apply lockout, call maintenance, and quarantine every part that was in the
machine. A collision can bend the spindle without visible damage.
