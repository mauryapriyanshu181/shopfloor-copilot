# Escalation Contacts and Rules

## When to escalate
Escalate when: a crack is detected, a dimensional deviation exceeds 0.12, three or more
defects appear in the same work order, or the inspection result conflicts with the ERP
work order status.

## Contacts
- Shift Supervisor: Anita Rao, ext 4120, covers all shifts, first point of contact.
- Quality Manager: D. Fernandes, ext 4101, scrap above 5 units or any customer-return lot.
- Maintenance Lead: ext 4155, for repeated dimensional deviation on the same machine,
  which indicates tool wear rather than a part problem.

## Response times
Crack or safety-related escalation: immediate, stop the line.
Dimensional or process escalation: within one shift.
